# Project retirement

**Officially concluded: 10 November 2026**  
**Final internal patch: v3.7.17-p11**

The original project's multi-addon aggregation model relied on upstream services that began introducing or planning stronger official-client device authentication. Outages and permissions further reduced the long-term viability of the design. The project was retired by its owner out of respect for upstream developers and to avoid dependence on techniques that could conflict with their access controls.

This archive preserves the general technical techniques, without implying the protected providers are accessible, supported, or endorsed. The retired infrastructure may be deleted separately; publishing this archive does not deactivate Cloudflare resources or revoke any keys.
